## Agentes — prompt utilizado

A single transparent PNG image, 1024 pixels wide by 1536 high. EXACT layout 4 columns by 6 rows, 24 equal 256x256 cells, no gutters, no drawn grid, NO text, NO labels. Every cell contains one centered full-body character with generous empty transparent padding, all feet/body centers aligned consistently. Camera top-down three-quarter overhead, faces oriented south toward viewer; crisp detailed pixel art like bespoke high-quality 32-bit pixel animation, not 3D renders, dark navy armor with clear luminous accents, readable at 48 pixels. Each ROW is ONE character across FOUR sequential walking cycle frames, same scale and costume, small coherent limb changes only, no direction changes: row1 Rexx Vektor, balanced armored explorer with pale mint visor, navy combat armor and compact pulse gauntlet; row2 Nyra Flux, slender female engineer blue short hair under helmet, cyan visor, compact mechanical backpack; row3 Brakk Ferrol, broad heavy soldier orange visor with thick amber-accent shoulder armor; row4 Suri Rastro, agile slim runner raspberry visor and short magenta trailing scarf; row5 Ilya Boreal, elemental researcher violet hood with icy cyan crystals on shoulders; row6 NUL-7, mysterious humanoid android white faceplate, charcoal segmented body, violet floating core. Consistent unified original design, no existing franchise references. Every character completely fits in its cell and occupies about 65% height. Real alpha transparency, NOT a checkerboard drawing, NOT a colored background. No scenery, no shadow outside each character, no logos.
